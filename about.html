About us

One company.
Two jobs done right.

Pentium Logistics was built around a simple idea: the businesses that ship goods out are the same businesses left with recyclable waste to clear — so we do both.

Our story

Freight and recycling,
under one roof

Most hauliers do one thing. Pentium runs two dedicated divisions side by side: Pentium Express, moving palletised freight business to business from Dublin out across the country, and Pentium Environmental, collecting recyclable waste with rigid trucks that carry their own Moffett mounted forklifts.

How the company fits together: Pentium Logistics is the operation as a whole. Under it sit two divisions — Pentium Express for palletised freight, and Pentium Environmental Logistics for recyclable waste. Fast Moves, the removals business, is a separate company run by the same people.

Keeping both under one operation means our customers deal with one company, one standard and one point of contact — whether it's stock going out the gate or baled cardboard coming off the yard.
