Two divisions of the one company. Open either one for the detail — what we carry, how it's collected and how it's booked.

Division 01

Pentium Express

B2B pallet distribution nationwide from our Dublin base — single pallets through to full loads.

The freight arm. We collect palletised goods from your premises and deliver them straight to your customers, with a confirmed collection window on every booking.

What's included

  • Pallet distribution — single pallet up to full load on scheduled runs.
  • Next-day & economy — choose the speed the job actually needs.
  • Groupage & part loads — pay for the space you use, not the truck.
  • Tail-lift & assisted offload — for sites with no dock or forklift.
  • Regular contract runs — fixed weekly routes to the same customers.

Division 02

Pentium Environmental Logistics

Recyclable waste collected from any site — our rigids carry their own Moffett mounted forklifts.

The recycling arm. Because the offload equipment travels with the truck, we can collect from yards and sites that have nothing on the ground — then deliver straight to licensed facilities.

What's included

  • Rigids with Moffett forklifts — self-sufficient collections, every time.
  • Baled cardboard & plastics — scheduled lifts direct from your site.
  • Site clearances — accumulated recyclables cleared in a single visit.
  • Delivery to recycling facilities — material moved on for processing.
  • Scheduled collections — weekly or fortnightly rounds, same day each week, sized to your volume.
